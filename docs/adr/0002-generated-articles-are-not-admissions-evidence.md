# Exclude generated articles from personal admissions evidence

Content that AI freely produces or substantially rewrites is not presented as evidence of the applicant's independent writing or academic understanding. Treating generated prose as a personal Note would make the automated workflow look smoother at the cost of misrepresenting authorship in an admissions-facing portfolio.


[ADR 0005](0005-issue-driven-generated-note-publication.md) permits clearly
attributed generated explanations in the personal Notes collection. They remain
separate from claims of independent student authorship or understanding.
