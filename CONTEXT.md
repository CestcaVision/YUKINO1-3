# Personal Learning and Work

A personal space for sharing learning, Notes, and Works over time. The homepage introduces this ongoing collection rather than presenting an admissions pitch. Existing evidence and authorship rules still apply to published content.

## Language

**Portfolio**:
The complete public body of evidence presented through the website.
_Avoid_: Personal homepage, résumé website

**Work**:
A completed or substantially demonstrable artifact created by the applicant, such as software, an animation, or an interactive experiment.
_Avoid_: Project idea, skill badge

**Planned Work Category**:
A kind of Work the applicant intends to create in the future; it is navigation planning, not evidence and not a Work.
_Avoid_: Work, upcoming project

**Case Study**:
The evidence-backed account of a Work's purpose, difficult decisions, process, result, and the applicant's own contribution.
_Avoid_: Project card, showcase blurb

**Capability**:
An ability demonstrated by one or more Works or Notes rather than asserted by the applicant without evidence.
_Avoid_: Skill rating, proficiency bar

**Medium**:
The form or tool through which a Work is expressed, such as a web experience, command-line program, 3D animation, or interactive application.
_Avoid_: Subject, capability

**Subject**:
The academic area addressed by a Work or Note, such as mathematics, economics, physics, or computer science.
_Avoid_: Medium, capability

**Note**:
An applicant-authored explanation that demonstrates understanding of a mathematical, economic, or related academic topic.
_Avoid_: AI article, content post

**Generated Article**:
An article whose argument or explanation may be freely produced or substantially rewritten by AI; it is not evidence of the applicant's independent writing or subject understanding.
_Avoid_: Note, applicant-authored work

**Source Brief**:
The applicant's concise original material submitted for developing a Note, containing the ideas or observations the applicant intends to communicate.
_Avoid_: Prompt, issue content

**Draft Note**:
An unpublished expansion of a Source Brief that may be AI-assisted and must be reviewed and approved by the applicant before publication.
_Avoid_: Published note, final article

**Academic Result**:
An achieved or officially predicted examination result, identified by qualification, subject where applicable, status, and date.
_Avoid_: Score placeholder, informal test mark

**Academic Snapshot**:
A compact selection of existing Academic Results that provides context without becoming the Portfolio's main evidence of capability.
_Avoid_: Transcript, empty score grid

**Recent Activity**:
A chronological view of newly published Works, Notes, and meaningful milestones.
_Avoid_: Commit feed, status update, social feed
